Design Principles

Consistency · Accessibility · Simplicity · Responsiveness

01
CONSISTENCY

One source of truth, one voice, and one product. Every screen, component, and interaction follows the same rules. Enforced across visual (same color tokens), behavioural (hover always gold, focus rings 2px), spatial (8px base unit), and typographic (H1 always 48px DM Sans Semibold) consistency.

02
ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility is a system property, not an afterthought. Embedded in every token, component, and guideline from day one. Adheres to WCAG 2.2 AA across four areas: Perceivable (4.5:1 contrast), Operable (keyboard reachable), Understandable (plain-language errors), and Robust (screen reader tested).

03
SIMPLICITY

Reduce cognitive load. Every element earns its place. No shadows by default, only three font weights (400, 500, 600), a limited colour palette, and clean typography with DM Sans's low stroke contrast. The simpler the interface, the longer the analyst can work effectively.

04
RESPONSIVENESS

Seamless adaptation across all screen sizes. On mobile: sidebar hides behind hamburger menu, metric cards stack vertically, tables scroll horizontally. On desktop: full sidebar, cards display three to four per row. Same content, same insights — different layouts.
